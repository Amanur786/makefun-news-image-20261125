# The World in One Page — 25 November 2026

A 1024×1024 newspaper-style poster that sums up Wednesday 25 November
2026's world news in a single image.

## What it shows

- **Lead story — Huawei found guilty in US trial:** a US jury convicts
  Huawei of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud.
- **Ukraine:** US-mediated talks resume in Abu Dhabi; a prisoner swap of
  1,500 per side is eyed as Kyiv faces a 27 November deadline.
- **Space:** China launches Shenzhou-24 carrying a Pakistani astronaut —
  the first foreign astronaut aboard China's space station.
- **Middle East:** Houthis strike Riyadh; the US warns Americans in the
  region of a heightened threat.
- **Tech:** Nvidia's Jensen Huang apologises to staff and says he is in
  "therapy" as a US Senate leader accuses him of breaking export law.
- **Sport:** Senegal win back the Africa Cup title after a CAS ruling.
- **Chess:** Gukesh holds Sindarov in the world championship match.
- **Also today:** Shein's $100m US settlement, a corruption probe into a
  Chinese Politburo member, a cyber breach at the CIA/ODNI, and India's
  call for human oversight of AI in warfare.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024×1024) next to `main.py`. Pure Pillow
(`Pillow >= 10.0.0`); no other dependencies, no network, no randomness —
the same run always draws the same page.
